01 — About

Turning drawings into coordinated, data-rich models.

I'm a BIM Specialist focused on architectural and MEP coordination across the full project lifecycle — from concept modeling through construction documentation and as-built handover. My work centers on clash detection, model federation, LOD management, and writing BIM Execution Plans (BEP) that keep project teams aligned.

I work daily in Revit, Navisworks Manage and AutoCAD, and use Dynamo to automate repetitive modeling tasks so teams can focus on design decisions instead of manual rework.

  • Architectural & MEP model coordination
  • Clash detection & resolution workflows
  • BIM Execution Plan (BEP) authoring
  • 4D/5D scheduling & quantity takeoff support
